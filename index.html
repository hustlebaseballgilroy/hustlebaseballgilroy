Who We Are

More Than a Team

Established in 2024, Hustle Baseball is a competitive youth travel baseball organization based in Gilroy, California — a pathway for youth baseball development beyond traditional little league programs.

01

Our Vision

To be the leading youth baseball organization that inspires excellence through sportsmanship and fosters personal and athletic growth.

02

Our Mission

To foster a love for youth baseball by cultivating a culture of sportsmanship, relentless hustle, and personal growth — while building community among players, families, and supporters.

03

Our Purpose

To empower young athletes through the game of baseball, teaching the values of sportsmanship, growth, and hustle while developing character and a lifelong passion for the sport.

For Parents

Got Questions?

The things families ask us most. Don't see yours? Email info@hustlebaseballgilroy.org.

How do I register for tryouts?

Registration is required. Email tryouts@hustlebaseballgilroy.org with your child's name and the age level they're trying out for, or use the online sign-up form.

Which division should my child try out for?

11U is for players born on or after May 1, 2015. 12U is for players born on or after May 1, 2014. The division checker in the tryouts section will tell you instantly.

What does my player need to bring?

A bat, helmet, glove, and cleats are all required.

Where are tryouts held?

Paradise Park softball field, 365 La Crosse Dr, Morgan Hill, CA 95037.

How is travel ball different from little league?

Hustle teams compete in regional tournaments. The program is a development pathway beyond traditional little league, built around fundamentals, sportsmanship, and hustle.

How can my business sponsor the team?

Sponsorships help cover uniforms, tournament fees, and equipment. Email info@hustlebaseballgilroy.org to talk about Platinum, Gold, and Silver tiers.

Ready to hustle?

Tryouts for the Summer/Fall 2026 season are open to 11U and 12U players. Reach out and our coaching staff will follow up with next steps.
